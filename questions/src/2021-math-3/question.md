다음 보기 중 finitely generated 하지 않은 group을 모두 고르시오.

1. The fundamental group of an open unit disk with finitely many punctured points.
2. $SL_2(\mathbb{Z})$; i.e., the group of $2\times2$ matrices with integer entries and determinant $1$.
3. $\mathbb{C}^{\times}$; i.e., the group of non-zero complex numbers with respect to multiplication.
4. Monster group; i.e., the largest sporadic finite simple group.
